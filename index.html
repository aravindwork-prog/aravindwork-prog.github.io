About me

I’m an M.Tech Integrated Software Engineering student at VIT Vellore who enjoys building software that is reliable, clear and genuinely useful. My interests sit across full-stack engineering and machine learning.

I have a solid foundation in Java, object-oriented programming, data structures and databases, and I turn it into working products with Spring Boot, React and MySQL. During my internship at enxcl Business Solutions I built a Resident Management System end to end, from normalized database design and REST APIs to the user interface, while working in an Agile team.

On the machine learning side, my final year project is a TabNet-based system for chronic kidney disease screening. It works from routine clinical parameters and shows which features drove each prediction, because a screening tool is only useful if the people relying on it can understand its reasoning. I have also worked on computer vision, using a CNN and OpenCV to tell genuine handwritten signatures from forgeries.

Away from the screen, I have played cricket for my district team, I enjoy badminton, and I was an active member of the Bharat Scouts and Guides during my school years. Sport and scouting taught me discipline, teamwork and responsibility, and I carry those habits into every project I join.
